# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Purpose
This README is the central index for OctoAcme's project management documentation. It gives new and existing teammates a single starting point for understanding how OctoAcme plans, delivers, and improves projects, with links to every process document in this folder.

## Overview of OctoAcme's Project Management Process
OctoAcme runs projects through a lightweight, iterative lifecycle grounded in customer value, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. A project starts with **initiation**, where the team confirms the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success criteria, and makes a go/no-go decision before moving forward. Approved initiatives move into **planning**, where the team breaks work into a prioritized, estimated backlog, defines a Definition of Done, maps dependencies and risks, and agrees on a release timeline.

During **execution and tracking**, the team follows a regular rhythm — daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and sprint demos — using a project board and small pull requests to move work from Backlog through Done while keeping status and decisions visible. Throughout the project, **risk management and communication** practices keep a risk register up to date and ensure stakeholders receive consistent updates and timely escalations. When work is ready to ship, the **release and deployment** process standardizes patch/minor/major release types, pre-release checks (acceptance criteria, CI, security scans, rollback plans), and post-release verification to reduce risk.

After a sprint, release, or milestone (and after incidents), the team holds a **retrospective** to capture what went well, what could improve, and concrete action items, feeding continuous improvement back into the next cycle. Throughout the lifecycle, well-defined **roles and personas** — Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers — collaborate with clear responsibilities and communication patterns to keep delivery predictable and transparent.
